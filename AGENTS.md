## Editorial writing

For new historical posts and substantial prose rewrites, use
`editorial/viktoria-posts/SKILL.md` and its original-post examples. Victoria's
existing channel voice is the reference; internal research briefs are not
publication-ready articles. Keep factual checks and source attribution while
writing for readers. Do not invent the author's personal experiences.

Before publishing a new historical piece or a substantial rewrite, have a
separate agent who did not author that version read the complete text. The
opening must establish who or what, where, when and the situation. Explain
unfamiliar names and terms on first mention when needed for understanding.
Check that the text develops its subject and reaches a concrete ending without
invented scenes or causes; an explanatory piece does not need manufactured
drama. Resolve the review findings and have the reviewer re-read the corrected
version. Keep a short working verdict identifying author, reviewer, version,
resolved findings and acceptance or remaining blockers. Author self-review does
not replace this gate. Accept the Russian source before updating translations.

For narrative articles, select real archival or original photographs and verify
the subject, source, usage rights and exact caption. Link the original record;
do not infer a photographer from an album compiler or report author. State any
material difference between a contextual image and the object in the story:
a photograph of another bank must not stand for the memoirist's workplace.
If no suitable photograph is found, record that in the working notes rather
than invent a documentary image. Follow the detailed workflow in the skill.

## Development

When starting the dev server, use background mode:

```
astro dev --background
```

Manage the background server with `astro dev stop`, `astro dev status`, and `astro dev logs`.
